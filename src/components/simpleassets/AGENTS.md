## Selection, transfer and bridge windows
- Bridging starts from the grid selection bar (beside Transfer/Burn) and BridgeDialog only confirms the selection; eligibility comes from getBridgeEligibility in bridgeActions.ts so the bar and dialog never disagree.
- Selected-card tiles in the transfer and bridge windows come from the shared SelectedAssetGrid (64px artwork, original mint number on top via getMintLabel) so both flows show the same card identity and mint as the grid ribbon.
- BridgeDialog bridges both ways via atomicbridge: SA->AA uses simpleassets::offer memo "swap" (contract claims inline); AA->SA uses atomicassets::transfer to atomicbridge (memo ignored, "unbridge" used descriptively). Only AA cards with immutable sassets_id may go back — mirrors the contract swap-record lookup. Cards are never burned; bridge holds custody. Max 20 cards per tx.
- Pack transfers send SimpleAssets token packs via packs.topps::transfer by quantity and AtomicAssets packs via atomicassets::transfer by asset ID (src/lib/packTransferActions.ts), and pack and card selections are mutually exclusive, because the two pack kinds are different on-chain objects and mixed card/pack batches would confuse the transfer window.
- After a successful transfer, burn, bridge or pack donation, hide sent IDs and cap token-pack counts via src/lib/pendingRemovals.ts until refetched data catches up, because indexers lag the chain and a plain refetch can still show what left the wallet.
- Replies to sent trade offers (accepted/declined/countered) are tracked in src/lib/tradeReplies.ts by diffing pending sent offers only after a successful poll, and offers resolved from this app (cancel, own counter) are marked self-resolved, because an empty list during loading or my own action must never look like a reply.
- Pack reveal windows start src/lib/collectionPreload.ts for the dealt collection (mirror-first, 6 at a time, grid bottom first) and never cancel it on close, because the deal animation scrolls that grid right after closing.

- Pair timeline bridge custody with this AA copy’s creation time and verified mint recipient in the shared history merger; show a separate AA receipt, never infer its recipient from a later sale/current owner, because bridge custody is not a gift.
- Use the shared history comparator for merging and display: same-time AA deposits into AtomicMarket precede sales, because accepted offers escrow and settle in one transaction; never alter timestamps.
- Price sales in USD by UTC day from the bundled src/data/wax-usd-daily.json (built by scripts/build-wax-usd.mjs) and CheeseHub token-price-data for newer days; omit USD when no day price exists, never estimate.
